# Preliminary CPS Results Snapshot

These are descriptive validation results **before** the LM-AIOE exposure merge.

## Analytical panel

- 11,635,165 Basic Monthly CPS person-records (2017-2025)
- 6,849,416 records age 18-64
- 4,344,203 at-work wage/salary records
- 3,456,082 full-time wage/salary records
- 1,112,384 eligible one-year baseline observations (2017-2024)
- 1,062,438 validated one-year transitions
- 13,103 FT → part-time-for-economic-reasons (PTER) transitions

## Pooled descriptive checks

- Clean pre-period (2017-18 baselines): 1.131% weighted FT→PTER
- COVID shock (2019 baseline): 2.848%
- Post-2022 (2022-24 baselines): 1.133%
- Overall weighted unemployment transition rate: 1.852%
- Overall weighted non-economic part-time transition rate: 7.258%
- Overall weighted remaining-full-time rate: 82.823%

The near-identical aggregate pre/post PTER rate does **not** reject the project hypothesis. The hypothesis is about **relative post-2022 divergence by occupation-level language-model exposure**, which requires the LM-AIOE merge.
